# Browse and open

- Expand a connection, then "Blob Containers", then a container. Folders come from the `/` in blob names.
- 1,000 items load at a time. Click "Load more" for the next page.
- Click a blob to open it. Text and JSON open in an editor tab; Save writes the blob back if nobody changed it in the meantime.
- Right-click a blob for "Properties, Tier and SAS URL", "Copy URL", "Generate SAS URL", "Change Access Tier", "Rename" and "Delete".
